SSEBYIGGA JOHN VIANNEY

Master of contemporary realism with over 20 years of dedicated practice in portrait artistry. Trained my-self the skill and influenced by the dramatic chiaroscuro of Caravaggio and the psychological depth of Lucian Freud.

My work hangs in private collections across Uganda and EastAfrica. My distinctive approach combines classical techniques with modern sensibilities, creating portraits that capture not just likeness, but essence.

Exhibitions: The Native Culture,Uganda Muesuem (2022)
